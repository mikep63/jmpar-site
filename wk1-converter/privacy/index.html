WK1 Converter

Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

WK1 Converter does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • It doesn't connect to the internet. Your spreadsheets never leave your Mac.

Your files

The app reads only the files you open or add to Convert Files. It writes only the files you ask for: an export goes where you choose in the save dialog, and Convert Files saves next to each original or in the folder you choose. It keeps no copies of your files and no settings. macOS keeps its usual list of recently opened files, which you can clear from File ▸ Open Recent.

Email

If you email support, your message and email address are used only to reply to you. They are not shared with anyone. If you attach a spreadsheet, it is used only to look into your problem and is deleted afterwards.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
