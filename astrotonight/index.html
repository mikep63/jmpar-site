DeepSky Tonight

Plan which deep-sky objects to photograph tonight, for iPhone and iPad.

Support

For questions or problems, email support@jmpar.com.

Where the data comes from

The object catalog is built on OpenNGC (CC BY-SA 4.0), with other catalogs from VizieR/CDS and Digitized Sky Survey images (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial). All of it is built into the app. The changes DeepSky Tonight makes and the source files are on the catalog data and license page, and full credits are in the app's About screen.
