Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

AstroTonight does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It is an astrophotography planning app, and this policy says plainly what it does with your data.

Location

AstroTonight asks for your device's location while you are using the app, to calculate which deep-sky objects are visible from where you are and when they rise, transit and set. The calculation happens entirely on your device. Your location is never sent anywhere and is never shared with anyone. If you save a location by name (for example "Home" or a dark-sky site), it is kept only in the app's local database on your device. You can decline location access and enter a saved site instead.

No accounts, no tracking

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • It makes no network requests. The catalogs and images are built into the app.

Your settings and logs

Your filters, favorites, saved sites and imaging log stay on your device. If you export a backup or a CSV, you choose where it goes using the iOS share sheet. The app does not send it anywhere.

Third-party data used to build the app

The deep-sky object catalog and images come from public astronomical sources (OpenNGC, the Sharpless catalog via VizieR/CDS, and the Digitized Sky Survey) and are bundled into the app at build time. Using the app does not send any request to these sources. The OpenNGC catalog is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; see catalog data and license for the source files and a list of modifications. The popular-target highlights are an editorial pick by the developer.

Children

AstroTonight does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information.

Email

If you email support, your message and email address are used only to reply to you. They are not shared with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
