Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Radio Spectrum does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • It doesn't connect to the internet. All frequency data is built into the app.
  • It doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, or any other data on your device.

Email

If you email support, your message and email address are used only to reply to you. They are not shared with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
