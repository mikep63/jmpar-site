Radio Spectrum

US radio frequency allocations from 8.3 kHz to 3 GHz, for iPhone and iPad.

Support

For questions, problems, or data corrections, email support@jmpar.com. If you're reporting a data error, please include the frequency range and what you expected to see.

Where the data comes from

Allocations come from the FCC's Table of Frequency Allocations (47 CFR § 2.106), FCC edition revised September 11, 2026. Federal allocations are taken from the Federal column of the same table. Footnote text comes from 47 CFR § 2.106 as of October 1, 2024, except where the app marks a footnote as taken from the FCC table PDF.

Service definitions come from 47 CFR § 2.1. Amateur license classes and modes come from 47 CFR Part 97. The channel lists (CB, FRS, GMRS, MURS, marine VHF and TV) come from 47 CFR Parts 95, 80 and 73. These come from the eCFR; the About screen in the app shows the date.

The app covers the United States only (ITU Region 2), and frequencies from 8.3 kHz to 3 GHz. It does not include the NTIA's own tables, amateur band plans, or other countries.

Important

Radio Spectrum is a reference tool. It is not an official source, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FCC or NTIA. Allocations change, and the app may not reflect the latest rules. Before you transmit or rely on an allocation for any regulatory purpose, check the current 47 CFR § 2.106 and the rules that apply to your license.
